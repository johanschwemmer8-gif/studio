# Pilot Store Deployment Guide

This document outlines the physical procedure for deploying iNteract digital activations in-store.

## 1. Pre-Deployment (Head Office)
- [ ] **Catalog Ready**: All pilot products added to the iNteract Product Catalog.
- [ ] **Branding Published**: Logo and template confirmed in Brand & Experience.
- [ ] **Activations Generated**: QR batches processed for each participating product.
- [ ] **Package Ready**: Deployment ZIP and Manifest downloaded.

## 2. Physical Preparation
1. **Printing**: Print the QR images from the package onto 40mm x 40mm adhesive labels.
2. **Sorting**: Use the `deployment_manifest.csv` to sort stickers by product name and barcode.
3. **Materials**: Ensure shelf-edge label (SEL) holders are clear and accessible.

## 3. Placement (In-Store)
1. **Match**: Confirm the physical product on the shelf matches the name and barcode in the manifest.
2. **Stick**: Place the QR sticker to the right of the price on the shelf-edge label.
3. **Position**: Ensure the QR is flat, unobstructed, and at a height comfortable for shopper scanning (eye level or chest level is ideal).

## 4. Mandatory Verification
Before the store opens or the pilot is declared "Live":
- **Scan 100%**: A member of the deployment team must scan every newly placed QR code.
- **Confirm**:
    - Branded experience loads within 3 seconds.
    - Correct product name and description are displayed.
    - Ari Assistant initializes with the correct product context.
- **Failure**: If a code resolves incorrectly, remove the sticker and report the QR ID to the Retailer Administrator for regeneration.

## 5. Staff Briefing
Store staff should be aware that:
- These codes are part of the iNteract Decision Intelligence pilot.
- They provide shoppers with expert buying guidance through the Ari AI assistant.
- Any damaged or missing stickers should be reported to the Store Manager immediately.
