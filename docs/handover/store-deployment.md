# Pilot Store Deployment Guide

## 1. Pre-Deployment Checklist
- [ ] Retailer Brand configuration published.
- [ ] At least 5 products activated in **QR Activation**.
- [ ] Test scan performed: QR resolves to correct brand and product.
- [ ] Analytics verified: Test scan appears in **Scan Statistics**.

## 2. Physical Placement
1. **Printing**: Use the PDF/PNG export from the QR Campaign Dashboard.
2. **Placement**: Place QR codes at eye level next to product price tags.
3. **Verification**: Store staff should perform one "Arrival Scan" per day to ensure connectivity.

## 3. Staff Activation
Encourage staff to use the **Checkout Sync (SIM)** tool to understand how digital baskets transition to the physical terminal.
